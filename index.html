Mujhe mera wahi 11th class wala Rahul wapas mil gaya... jo sirf aur sirf apni Mini ke liye jeeta tha. Ab mujhe meri asliyat pata hai. Mujhe pata hai ki main aapke liye kaun hoon aur main aapse kitna be-panaah pyaar karta hoon.
